# Reza Zamani, Ph.D.

**Quantitative Researcher | Financial Machine Learning | Economist**

🌐 **Website:** [rezazamani2329.github.io](https://rezazamani2329.github.io)

I am a quantitative researcher with a Ph.D. in Economics, completing the Master of Financial Engineering (MFE) at UC Berkeley Haas. I build models that turn large financial datasets into signals, forecasts and decisions, with work in asset pricing, financial machine learning, intraday futures, fixed income and structured finance.

## About this repository

This repository hosts my personal website, published with GitHub Pages. It brings together:

- **Experience:** quantitative research (BlackRock and Franklin Templeton MFE industry projects), data science and AI engineering, securities issuance, and ten years as a professor of economics
- **Education:** UC Berkeley MFE; Stanford AI Professional Program; UC Berkeley Professional Certificate in Machine Learning and AI; MIT Applied Generative AI for Digital Transformation; Ph.D. in Economics
- **Selected projects:** macroeconomic surprises in index futures, mortgage credit risk (Freddie Mac STACR), conditional lead-lag alpha, deep learning in asset pricing, FX and crypto carry, high-frequency trading signals, VaR backtesting
- **Papers and publications:** journal articles and working papers in macroeconomics, political economy, public debt and quantitative finance
- **Certificates:** Columbia, Yale, UIUC, HKUST, UC Berkeley and others, with verification links
