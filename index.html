About me

I am a quantitative researcher with a Ph.D. in Economics, completing the Master of Financial Engineering at UC Berkeley Haas. I build models that turn large financial datasets into signals, forecasts and decisions.

My work spans asset pricing, financial machine learning, intraday futures and fixed income. Through Berkeley MFE industry projects with BlackRock and Franklin Templeton, I applied these methods to real investment questions, and I am developing a working paper on the signal in macroeconomic releases.

Before the MFE, I taught macroeconomics at the Ph.D. and Master's levels, supervised dissertations in finance and forecasting, and worked on fiscal and monetary policy analysis. I also managed securities issuance end to end at Central Asset Management Company and served as Director of Budgeting for a university of more than 15,000 students. I have worked as a data scientist and AI engineer, and I have trained in AI at Stanford (AI Professional Program, in progress), MIT Professional Education (Applied Generative AI for Digital Transformation) and UC Berkeley (Professional Certificate in Machine Learning and AI).
